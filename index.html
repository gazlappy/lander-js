How to fly

Mouse / WASDTilt the ship, exactly as in the 1987 original: how far the mouse is from the centre sets the tilt, the direction sets which way (R recentres)
Left button / Space / ZFull thrust
Middle button / ShiftHover (gentle thrust, as the original). H toggles an optional hover hold that keeps your height
Right button / F / XFire
C / MSwitch camera / enlarge the map
Esc / PPause

Touch down gently (the LAND light turns green) and level. Sit on a friendly pad to refuel and repair. Missions earn credits to buy new craft in the hangar.

How to play

Mouse positionTilt the ship: how far from the centre sets the tilt, the direction sets which way
Left button / ZFull thrust
Middle button / SpaceHover
Right button / XFire (costs a point per shot)
Esc / PPause and open the menu

Game speed, resolution, colours and mouse speed are in Options. Your game is saved when you pause or leave the page, so Continue picks up where you left off.

Shoot trees and buildings for 20 points each. Land gently on the grey launchpad to refuel. Score 800 or more and rocks start falling from the sky, and gravity gets stronger at 1024 and 1488.

Two takes on David Braben's Lander (Acorn Archimedes, 1987): a faithful retro remake and a modern open-world version. Written with the help of Mark Moxon's fully documented source.
